# Spines from photographs — summary

65 photographs of Umberto Eco's library (Milan apartment and the 2026 Bologna reconstruction) were reviewed. 77 individual titles/authors were transcribed from legible spines or title pages (42 high-confidence, 23 medium, 12 low); the remaining ~6,045 visible books across all shelf rows are recorded as unlabelled counts because their spine text could not be resolved even after digital upscaling — most of this collection is photographed at a resolution below what individual spine text requires (whole-wall surveys served at 650-1400px), and in one case (the Zanni 2010 portrait, full resolution) the books are lying open on display rather than shelved spine-out. Full detail is in `spines_photos.jsonl`.
